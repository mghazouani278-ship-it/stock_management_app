Request account deletion

Egypt Grid — Stock & Project Management

You can request deletion of your user account and associated personal data. Business records required for legal or accounting purposes may be retained by your organization administrator.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the address linked to your account to admin@egypt-grid.com.
  2. Subject: Account deletion request — Egypt Grid app
  3. Include your full name and the email used to sign in.
  4. We will confirm your identity and process the request within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login account (email, name, role assignment).
  • Personal identifiers linked only to your user profile.

What may be kept

  • Orders, stock movements, and project records created in a business context may be retained by Egypt Grid for operational and legal compliance, with personal references removed where applicable.
  • Server logs may be kept for a limited period for security.
